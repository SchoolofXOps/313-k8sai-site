RUNTIMECLASS-HANDLER-NO-FALLBACK​

A Pod naming a RuntimeClass that does not exist is refused at admission ("pod rejected: RuntimeClass ... not found"). A Pod whose RuntimeClass names a handler the node's containerd does not configure is scheduled but never starts: sandbox creation fails with no runtime for "<handler>" is configured, the kubelet records FailedCreatePodSandBox and retries, and the Pod stays in ContainerCreating. Nothing falls back to the default runtime; only a Pod with no runtimeClassName (nil or empty) resolves to the empty handler, which containerd maps to its default runtime.
